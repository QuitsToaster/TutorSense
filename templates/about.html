About TutorSense

TutorSense is an AI-powered student risk predictor. It analyzes attendance, homework, participation, and exam performance to identify students at risk of failing. Teachers can intervene early to provide targeted support.

Built with Python, Flask, Tailwind CSS, Chart.js, and a Random Forest machine learning model.
